# Contributor workflow

Research, inspection, planning, administration and ordinary notes may proceed without a task. Before changing implementation, agent instructions or enforcement, enter the [canonical contributor procedure](docs/runtime/contributor-workflow.md). When starting or resuming an issue, follow that procedure before implementation. For an assigned step thread, follow its bounded assignment and the procedure’s step-thread route.

When creating or updating a PR, follow the [PR preparation and verification rules](docs/runtime/contributor-workflow.md#pr-preparation-and-verification) and load the repository's [PR template](.github/pull_request_template.md).
